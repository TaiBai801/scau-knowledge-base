## 一、仔细阅读 Passage 1（环境 / 城市）

**文章（约 420 词）**

For much of the twentieth century, cities were planned around the car. Wide roads sliced through neighbourhoods, parking consumed vast tracts of land, and the measure of a successful street was how quickly traffic could pass through it. Trees, where they survived, were treated as decoration—a softening of concrete rather than a piece of essential infrastructure. A growing body of evidence now suggests this ordering was exactly backwards.

Urban trees turn out to be remarkably hard-working public assets. Their shade lowers surface temperatures on hot days, reducing the energy buildings spend on air conditioning and, in the process, easing the "heat island" effect that makes city centres several degrees warmer than surrounding countryside. Their roots absorb storm water that would otherwise flood streets, and their leaves filter a share of the fine particles that damage human lungs. Economists who have tried to price these services find that a mature street tree routinely returns several times its planting and maintenance cost.

The benefits are not only physical. Studies consistently link a view of greenery to lower stress, faster recovery in hospitals and better concentration in the classroom. Some researchers argue that trees even dampen crime: neighbourhoods with denser canopies tend to report fewer incidents, perhaps because well-tended streets signal that a community is watched and cared for.

Yet the very cities that most need trees are often the least able to grow them. Low-income districts are typically hotter and more polluted, yet they also tend to have the thinnest tree cover, a legacy of underinvestment that stretches back decades. When a heat wave arrives, it is these same neighbourhoods—with their exposed asphalt and ageing residents—that suffer first.

Closing that gap is not simply a matter of planting more saplings. Young trees take years to provide meaningful shade, and in compacted urban soil many fail within a season or two. The more durable solution is to protect the large, established trees that already exist, which means resisting the pressure to widen roads or to remove a healthy tree at the first sign of a cracked pavement. A century-old tree cannot be replaced by a dozen seedlings, no matter how many are planted.

**Q1.** What does the author mean by saying the twentieth-century ordering was "exactly backwards"?

A. Cities were built with too many cars and too few roads.
B. Trees were treated as decoration rather than essential infrastructure.
C. Parking spaces were placed where parks should have been.
D. Streets were designed without considering pedestrians.

**答案 B**　解析：首段末句"Trees... were treated as decoration—a softening of concrete rather than a piece of essential infrastructure"是"backwards"的具体所指——把树当装饰而非关键基础设施，方向反了。B 直接对应。
**易错点**：backwards 指"树与基础设施的主次被颠倒"，不是"路太少"或"车位位置"。

**Q2.** Which of the following is mentioned as an economic benefit of urban trees?

A. They increase the value of nearby housing.
B. They reduce the cost of building new roads.
C. They lower energy use for air conditioning.
D. They create jobs in landscape maintenance.

**答案 C**　解析：第二段"shade... reduces the energy buildings spend on air conditioning"，且"returns several times its planting and maintenance cost"。C 对应。A、B、D 未提。
**易错点**：economic 是关键词，锁定第二段"投入产出比"那段，勿选第一段的物理描述。

**Q3.** The author mentions lower crime in neighbourhoods with denser canopies to ______.

A. show that trees have benefits beyond the physical
B. prove that greenery directly prevents crime
C. argue that police should plant more trees
D. suggest that criminals avoid well-lit streets

**答案 A**　解析：第三段首句"The benefits are not only physical"，随后举减压、康复、专注、降低犯罪，都是"超越物理层面"的社会/心理效益。A 正确。B 的"directly"过度；C、D 曲解。
**易错点**：作者用"perhaps because"表推测，不是断言因果，勿选 B。

**Q4.** What is the situation in low-income districts described in the passage?

A. They enjoy abundant but poorly maintained trees.
B. They are hotter and more polluted yet have the least tree cover.
C. They have rejected urban greening programmes.
D. They suffer from excessive shade and dampness.

**答案 B**　解析：第四段"Low-income districts are typically hotter and more polluted, yet they also tend to have the thinnest tree cover"。B 为原文。
**易错点**：yet 表转折，正确选项要同时包含"更热更脏"和"树更少"两层。

**Q5.** What is the author's recommendation for closing the tree gap?

A. Plant as many young saplings as possible.
B. Widen roads to make space for new trees.
C. Protect existing large trees rather than only planting new ones.
D. Remove old trees that damage pavements.

**答案 C**　解析：末段"the more durable solution is to protect the large, established trees that already exist"。C 对应。A 被"not simply a matter of planting more saplings"否定；B、D 与作者主张相反。
**易错点**：作者说"resisting the pressure to widen roads or remove a healthy tree"，B、D 正是他要反对的。

## 二、仔细阅读 Passage 2（消费 / 社会）

**文章（约 400 词）**

The fashion industry has long sold the idea that a new wardrobe each season is both desirable and affordable. What it does not advertise is the price paid elsewhere: a single cotton T-shirt can require thousands of litres of water, and the dyeing of textiles is among the largest industrial polluters of clean water on the planet. For decades this accounting remained invisible, but it is becoming harder to ignore as the volume of clothing produced each year continues to climb.

The logic of the system is simple and brutal. Garments are made cheaply, worn a few times and discarded quickly, only to be replaced by more of the same. This "fast fashion" model depends on a constant cycle of production and disposal, in which the environmental cost is deferred to communities far from the shops where the clothes are sold. Consumers in wealthy countries enjoy low prices, while the burden of waste and pollution falls on regions they will never visit.

A counter-movement has emerged, urging consumers to buy less, choose better and make things last. Its champions point to the rise of repair cafés, clothing libraries and second-hand platforms as evidence that habits can change. The appeal is moral as much as practical: buying a durable garment and caring for it is framed not as sacrifice but as a small act of resistance against a system built on waste.

Sceptics, however, note that individual choices can only go so far. Even a determined consumer faces a market in which cheap, low-quality clothing is the default, while durable alternatives are scarce and expensive. They argue that the real lever lies with producers and regulators—through taxes on virgin materials, rules requiring products to be repairable, and transparency about where and how goods are made. Without such changes, they warn, the ethics of consumption will remain a matter of personal virtue rather than structural reform.

The two views are less opposed than they appear. Personal habits and policy change reinforce each other: visible consumer demand for durability gives regulators cover to act, while regulation that makes waste expensive reshapes the choices available to everyone. The question is not whether individuals or institutions should act, but how the two can be made to pull in the same direction.

**Q1.** What does the opening paragraph suggest about the fashion industry?

A. It has successfully lowered the price of clothing.
B. It hides the environmental cost of its products.
C. It is the world's largest consumer of cotton.
D. It is becoming more transparent about production.

**答案 B**　解析：首段"What it does not advertise is the price paid elsewhere"——行业不宣传产品背后的环境代价。B 对应。A 是背景不是主旨；D 与"remained invisible"相反。
**易错点**：not advertise = 隐藏，勿被"affordable"带偏选 A。

**Q2.** What is the core feature of the "fast fashion" model?

A. Rapid production and disposal on a constant cycle.
B. The use of cheap labour in developing countries.
C. The sale of clothes through online platforms.
D. The production of durable, high-quality garments.

**答案 A**　解析：第二段"depends on a constant cycle of production and disposal"——快速生产、快速丢弃的循环。A 直接对应。B、C 未提；D 与"低质"相反。
**易错点**：fast 指向"快产快弃"，不是"低价"或"线上"。

**Q3.** The counter-movement encourages consumers to ______.

A. stop buying new clothes altogether
B. buy less, choose better and make things last
C. boycott all major fashion brands
D. donate their old clothes to charity

**答案 B**　解析：第三段"urging consumers to buy less, choose better and make things last"。B 为原文。
**易错点**：counter-movement 主张"少买、买好、用久"，不是"完全不买"（A 极端）。

**Q4.** Why do sceptics believe individual choices are insufficient?

A. Consumers lack the moral awareness to change.
B. Repair cafés are too expensive for most people.
C. The market defaults to cheap, low-quality clothing.
D. Governments refuse to tax the fashion industry.

**答案 C**　解析：第四段"a market in which cheap, low-quality clothing is the default, while durable alternatives are scarce and expensive"——市场默认廉价低质，耐用选择稀缺昂贵。C 对应。
**易错点**：sceptics 不否认消费者意愿，而是指出市场结构使个体无力，勿选 A。

**Q5.** What is the author's overall view on the two camps?

A. Personal habit and policy change should reinforce each other.
B. Regulation is the only effective solution to fast fashion.
C. Individual virtue is more important than structural reform.
D. The two positions are fundamentally irreconcilable.

**答案 A**　解析：末段"Personal habits and policy change reinforce each other"，两者应同向发力。A 对应。B、C 偏执一端；D 与"less opposed than they appear"相反。
**易错点**：作者明确说两者"不是更对立"，末句落脚在"如何让两者同向"，选 A。

## 三、段落匹配（科技伦理 / 人工智能）

**文章（A–I 九段）**

A. The rapid spread of artificial intelligence into hiring, lending and criminal justice has turned a once-philosophical question into a practical one: can a machine be fair? The difficulty is that fairness, in the algorithmic sense, is not one thing but several, and the different definitions frequently contradict one another.

B. One notion of fairness demands that a system treat similar individuals similarly. If two applicants differ only in a protected attribute such as gender, they should receive the same decision. This sounds straightforward until one asks what counts as "similar," a question that leads quickly into deep water.

C. A second notion demands equality of outcomes: the proportion of people approved should be the same across groups. But enforcing this can require a system to apply different rules to different people, which violates the first notion. An algorithm cannot always satisfy both at once—a result researchers call the impossibility of fairness.

D. Part of the problem is that bias often enters long before any model is built. If historical data reflect past discrimination, a model trained on that data will learn to reproduce it. Cleaning the data helps, but it is rarely clear which patterns in the past were legitimate and which were not.

E. Transparency offers limited comfort. A model can be made explainable in the sense that its rules are visible, yet still produce outcomes that strike people as unjust. Explaining why a loan was denied does not, by itself, make the denial fair.

F. Some propose human oversight as the answer, keeping a person in the loop to review important decisions. But humans have biases of their own, and research shows they often defer to an algorithm's recommendation even when they believe it is wrong—a tendency known as automation bias.

G. Others argue that the problem is not the algorithm but the task it is asked to perform. Using machine learning to predict who will commit a crime, for example, may be misguided in principle, because such predictions are as much about policing as about people.

H. Despite these difficulties, progress is possible. Teams that state their fairness goals explicitly, test for a range of harms before deployment and monitor outcomes after release do measurably better than those that treat fairness as an afterthought.

I. The lesson is that fairness in AI is not a box to be ticked but a continuing commitment. It requires asking hard questions about data, definitions and deployment—and accepting that some of those questions have no final answer.

**题目（从 A–I 段中选一段作答）**

**1.** The argument that a task itself, not the algorithm, may be the real problem.

**答案 G**　解析：G 段提出"the problem is not the algorithm but the task it is asked to perform"，并举例预测犯罪。

**2.** A finding that humans tend to trust an algorithm even when they doubt it.

**答案 F**　解析：F 段提到"automation bias"——人即使认为算法错了，也常听从其建议。

**3.** The contradiction between treating similar people alike and equalising group outcomes.

**答案 C**　解析：C 段指出第二定义"结果均等"会违反第一定义"同案同判"，二者无法同时满足。

**4.** The point that bias can be inherited from historical data.

**答案 D**　解析：D 段说明若历史数据含歧视，模型会"learn to reproduce it"。

**5.** The claim that explaining a decision does not make it fair.

**答案 E**　解析：E 段指出可解释不等于公正，"Explaining why... does not make the denial fair"。

**6.** The idea that fairness has multiple, mutually conflicting definitions.

**答案 A**　解析：A 段指出"fairness is not one thing but several, and the different definitions frequently contradict"。

**7.** Evidence that careful teams perform better than those that ignore fairness.

**答案 H**　解析：H 段说明明确目标、测试、监测的团队"do measurably better"。

**8.** The view that fairness requires ongoing effort rather than a one-time check.

**答案 I**　解析：I 段总结"fairness in AI is not a box to be ticked but a continuing commitment"。

**9.** The question of what it means for two people to be "similar."

**答案 B**　解析：B 段提出"similar individuals"的定义难题——什么算"相似"。

**10.** The suggestion that keeping a human in the loop has its own limitations.

**答案 F**　解析：F 段指出人类审核者也有偏见，且存在 automation bias。

## 四、选词填空（压力 / 心理）

**文章（15 选 10）**

Stress is often spoken of as a modern disease, something to be eliminated wherever possible. Yet a closer look at the research suggests a more (1)____ picture. Not all stress is harmful; a short burst of it before a performance can sharpen attention and improve results. What separates helpful stress from its destructive cousin is not the (2)____ of pressure but its duration and the sense of control one has over it.

The body's stress response is designed to be brief. When a challenge appears, hormones such as adrenaline (3)____ the heart rate and focus energy on the task at hand. Once the challenge passes, the body returns to baseline. Problems arise only when the system never switches off. Chronic stress, in which the alarm stays on for weeks or months, wears down nearly every organ, from the heart to the immune system.

One of the most reliable buffers against this damage is a sense of control. Studies show that when people are given even a small (4)____ of choice over how they perform a stressful task, their physiological response is measurably milder. The perception that one can act, it seems, (5)____ the threat. This helps explain why the same workload can feel crushing to one person and (6)____ to another.

Recovery matters as much as exposure. The body repairs itself during rest, and people who (7)____ time for genuine relaxation—not the passive scrolling that passes for it—recover faster and stay healthier. Physical activity, deep breathing and time in nature all serve as active (8)____ that signal the brain the danger has passed.

The practical conclusion is therefore not to (9)____ all stress, which is impossible, but to manage its shape: to keep acute stress acute, to build islands of control and rest into each day, and to remember that the goal is not a life without pressure but a body that can (10)____ from it.

**选项（A–O，15 选 10）**

A. intensity　B. measured　C. balanced　D. recover　E. raises
F. avoid　G. degree　H. remedies　I. manageable　J. reduces
K. reserve　L. suppress　M. accurate　N. costly　O. shift

**答案：**
1. C（balanced）　2. A（intensity）　3. E（raises）　4. G（degree）　5. J（reduces）
6. I（manageable）　7. K（reserve）　8. H（remedies）　9. F（avoid）　10. D（recover）

**解析：**
1. **C balanced**：a more + 形容词 + picture；"更平衡/全面的图景"，与后文"并非所有压力都有害"呼应。measured（谨慎的）、accurate（准确的）语义不贴。
2. **A intensity**：the + 名词 + of pressure；"区别在于压力的强度还是持续时间与掌控感"，后文 duration 并列提示此空是"强度"。
3. **E raises**：adrenaline + 动词第三人称单数；"肾上腺素提高心率"，raise heart rate。
4. **G degree**：a small + 名词 + of；"哪怕一点点选择权"，a small degree of choice。
5. **J reduces**：主语 perception，宾语 the threat；"掌控感削弱威胁感"，reduce the threat。
6. **I manageable**：feel crushing to one and ____ to another；与 crushing（压垮的）对立的是 manageable（可应对的）。
7. **K reserve**：people who ____ time for relaxation；"为真正放松预留时间"，reserve time。
8. **H remedies**：active + 名词复数；"运动、深呼吸、亲近自然都是积极的疗愈手段"，remedies。
9. **F avoid**：not to ____ all stress；"不是要回避所有压力"，avoid。
10. **D recover**：a body that can ____ from it；"能从中恢复的身体"，recover from。

**易错点**：第 2 题勿因后文 duration 选"持续时间"，空格讲的是压力"大小/强度"（intensity）与持续时间并列；第 6 题 crushing 与 manageable 是一对反义，勿选 costly（昂贵的）。

## 五、写作（Directions 标准六级要求）

**题目：** For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay on the importance of lifelong learning. You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words.

**参考范文（约 180 词）：**

For most of human history, education was understood as a stage of life—something completed before work began. That model no longer holds. In a world where knowledge becomes outdated within years, learning can no longer stop at graduation; it must become a lifelong habit.

The first reason is economic. Automation and technological change continuously rewrite the skills that employment demands, and workers who stop learning risk being left behind. Lifelong learning is therefore less a luxury than a form of security. The second reason is personal. Learning keeps the mind active and engaged, and studies link continued intellectual activity in later life to slower cognitive decline. A curious mind, quite simply, is a healthier mind.

This does not mean everyone must return to formal study. Lifelong learning can take the form of reading, of acquiring a new skill, or of simply staying open to ideas that challenge one's assumptions. What matters is the attitude: the willingness to admit that there is always more to know. In the end, the most successful people are not those who learned the most in school, but those who never stopped learning after leaving it.

**评分要点（供自评）：**
- 破题：从"教育=人生阶段"的旧观念切入，引出"终身学习=习惯"
- 论证两条：经济安全 + 身心健康，由外而内递进
- 转折段说明"不必回校"，落到"态度"这一核心
- 结尾对比升华（learned most in school vs never stopped learning）

**易失分点**：把 lifelong learning 写成"活到老学到老"的口号堆砌；只讲工作不讲个人成长；缺乏具体形式举例。

## 六、翻译（汉译英）

**原文：** 二十四节气是中国古人根据太阳运行规律总结出的一套时间知识体系，用来指导农业生产。它把一年分为二十四个时段，每个节气都有鲜明的气候特征和农事安排。例如，"惊蛰"意味着春雷始鸣、万物复苏，"霜降"则提醒人们秋收已尽、寒冬将至。2016年，二十四节气被列入联合国教科文组织人类非物质文化遗产代表作名录，成为世界认识中国农耕文明的一扇窗口。

**参考译文：**

The twenty-four solar terms are a time-knowledge system that ancient Chinese people developed according to the movement of the sun, and they were used to guide agricultural production. They divide a year into twenty-four periods, each with distinctive climatic features and farming arrangements. For example, "Jingzhe" (Awakening of Insects) means that spring thunder begins to sound and all living things revive, while "Shuangjiang" (Frost's Descent) reminds people that the autumn harvest is over and the cold winter is approaching. In 2016, the twenty-four solar terms were inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity, becoming a window through which the world comes to understand China's farming civilization.

**采分点：**
- "根据太阳运行规律" → according to the movement of the sun
- "被列入……名录" → were inscribed on ... the List（被动语态）
- "成为世界认识……的一扇窗口" → becoming a window through which the world comes to understand ...
- 节气名音译 + 括号意译（Jingzhe (Awakening of Insects)）是通用做法
- "春雷始鸣、万物复苏" → spring thunder begins to sound and all living things revive（两个并列谓语）

**易错点**：整体时态——描述客观事实用一般现在时，末句 2016 年事件用一般过去时（were inscribed）；"非物质文化遗产"用 intangible cultural heritage，勿漏 intangible。
